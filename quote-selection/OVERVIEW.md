Quote selection lets you reference part of a conversation without copying it. Select text in an agent's messages and a small Quote button appears above the selection. Click it, or press Q while it is shown, and the selection lands in that pane's prompt as a Markdown quote, with the cursor placed right after it so you can type your question.

The quote is appended after anything already in the prompt. Selections inside text fields are ignored.

It works in the desktop app and the browser client. It does nothing on iOS and Android, where text selection goes through the system menu. Nothing leaves your machine: the plugin only reads the selection and writes to the prompt.
