Quote selection lets you comment on any part of a conversation, the way you review a document, and send those comments to the agent with your next message.

Select text in a chat and a small toolbar appears. Click Comment, or press Q, and write what you want to say about the passage; Enter saves it, Shift+Enter adds a line, Escape cancels, and clicking outside keeps what you typed. Copy puts the selection on the clipboard.

Each waiting comment tints its passage and gets a numbered blue badge. Click a badge to edit or delete the comment. An "N annotations" pill above the message box lists them, with Clear all, and Send now to send them right away. Otherwise they go out with the next message you send, one block per passage with the quoted text and your comment, and disappear once sent.

Comments are saved on the agent itself, on the daemon, so they survive a reload and go away when the agent is archived. Nothing else leaves your machine.

Selecting, badges and attaching comments to your message work in the desktop app and the browser. On iOS and Android the pill and its panel work, but text selection goes through the system menu. Paseo has no plugin API for chat selections, so the plugin reads the app's page markup and a later Paseo release can break it until it is updated. Requires Paseo 0.16 or later.
